1. Who is responsible for the service?

DealRank is responsible for operating the DealRank website and related online service.

The contact details of the person or legal entity responsible for the service will be provided on this website before the service is offered commercially.

2. What information do we collect?

When you submit a listing, DealRank may process information required to operate the service, including:

  • Business or listing name
  • Website URL
  • Description of the listing
  • Amount associated with the listing
  • Payment/order information necessary to confirm the transaction
  • Date and status of the transaction

Payment information is processed by PayPal. DealRank does not request or store your PayPal password or complete payment-card credentials.

3. Public information

Information submitted as part of a paid listing may be displayed publicly on the DealRank leaderboard, including the listing name, description, website URL and ranking/payment amount as applicable to the service.

Do not submit personal or confidential information in a listing unless you have a lawful reason to do so and understand that the information may become publicly visible.

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate the DealRank service
  • Display and rank listings

  • Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

5. Legal basis

Depending on the processing activity, personal data may be processed because it is necessary to provide a service requested by the user, to comply with legal obligations, to protect the legitimate interests of the service, or where consent is required and has been obtained.

6. Payment provider

Payments are processed by PayPal. When you make a payment, PayPal may process personal and payment information in accordance with PayPal's own privacy terms and policies.

DealRank only processes the information necessary to identify and confirm the transaction and provide the purchased service.

7. Hosting and technical services

These providers may process technical information necessary to deliver and secure the service.

8. Data retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain business and transaction records, prevent abuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

12. Third-party websites

DealRank listings may contain links to external websites. DealRank is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

13. Children

The service is intended for users who have the legal capacity required to use it. We do not knowingly request personal data from children where doing so would be prohibited by applicable law.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service, technology or applicable legal requirements change.
